Project Overview
This project is a multi-page responsive website created for Animal Rescue, a local non-profit organisation that rescues, rehabilitates and rehomes abandoned and unwanted dogs and cats.  
The website aims to increase adoptions, foster placements, donations and volunteer recruitment through a clean, accessible and user-friendly online presence.

Website Goals and Objectives

- Increase animal adoptions and foster applications
- Make it easy for users to donate and volunteer
- Improve online visibility and reach beyond social media
- Number of visitors within 2 months

Key Performance Indicators
- Number of adoption/foster form submissions
- Online donation enquiries
- Volunteer applications
- Website traffic and time on site

Key Features and Functionality
- Homepage with clear calls-to-action
- About Us page (history, mission, vision, team)
- Animals for Adoption page with photo gallery
- Contact page with multiple locations, map and contact form
- Consistent navigation across all pages

A simple website for a local non-profit animal rescue organisation based in Pretoria.

About the Project
-This is the website for **Pet Care** (also referred to as The Animal Rescue), a local non-profit organisation founded in Pretoria.  
The organisation rescues abandoned and unwanted pets such as dogs, cats, and rabbits around the city.

 Mission
-The mission of the Animal Rescue is to provide a safe haven for abandoned and neglected animals, while also promoting responsible pet ownership and animal welfare.  

The vision is to create a community where every animal is treated with compassion and respect, and where no animal is left behind.

Our Services
We offer a range of services to help animals in need, including:

- Rescue and rehabilitation of abandoned and neglected animals

- Spay and neuter programs to control the pet population
- Education and outreach programs to promote responsible pet ownership
