About Us page

This Is a local non-profit organisation which was published last year In Pretoria. The Animal rescue saves abandoned, unwanted pets such as dogs, cats, and rabbits around the city

What is its mission?

The mission of the Animal Rescue is to provide a safe haven for abandoned and neglected animals, while also promoting responsible pet ownership and animal welfare. The vision is to create a community where every animal is treated with compassion and respect, and where no animal is left behind.>
